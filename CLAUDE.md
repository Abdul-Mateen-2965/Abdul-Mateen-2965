# Rules from my corrections

These come from corrections I gave Claude between 9 and 14 November 2026.
Follow them in every session. The count shows how many times I said each one.

## How to work with me

1. **Go step by step, one thing at a time.** (3 times: 9 Nov, 10 Nov, 10 Nov)
   Do not jump ahead to the next section until I say the current one is done.
   My words: "Don't jump forward. We will go step by step."

2. **Answer the exact question I asked.** (about 4 times: 9 Nov, 10 Nov)
   When you give click-by-click steps, they must match what I see in Chrome on
   desktop. If I say a button is not shown, give a different path, do not
   repeat the same steps.

3. **Do not remove anything from my profile, CV or banner without asking me first.** (2 times: 10 Nov)
   If you think something should go, say what and why, then wait for my answer.

4. **Keep your ratings consistent.** (1 time: 10 Nov)
   If you rated something 8 and then improved it, do not score it lower later
   unless you explain exactly what changed.

5. **Give me the complete text, in a form I can copy easily.** (2 times: 9 Nov, 10 Nov)
   No partial drafts and no "rest stays the same".
